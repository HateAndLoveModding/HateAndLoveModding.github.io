It was not a portfolio exercise looking for a problem, it replaced an actual recurring chore, which is why the framing here is the stakeholder and not the code.

## What it actually replaced

The workflow before this tool involved manually processing incoming emails to pull out and filter CSV data, by hand, on a recurring basis. Automating that with Python on Linux saved the stakeholder more than 8 hours a year and improved consistency for the recurring data retrieval, since a script does not skip a step or make a copy-paste mistake the way a repeated manual process eventually does.

## Why this one is on the site

A tool that saves a real person real time on a task they actually had is a more honest signal of engineering judgment than an algorithmically flashier project nobody asked for, and it reads better with the real-stakeholder framing than with cleverness.
